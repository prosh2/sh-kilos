# Kilos

A social training app for endurance athletes. Post a **Kilo** for your next run, ride, or swim; find people at your pace; train together.

> Early development. See [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md).

## Stack

Expo (React Native, TypeScript) · Supabase (Postgres + PostGIS, Auth, Realtime)
